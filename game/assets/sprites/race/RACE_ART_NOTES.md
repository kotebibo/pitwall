# Race art notes: pit stops, pit lane, start, podium, race HUD

Everything here is generated by `python tools/art/race.py`. Edit the script and re-run it; don't hand-edit the PNGs. Every random choice is seeded, so a rebuild gives the same pixels. The script reads `car/*.png` and `track/asphalt.png` for its previews only.

Contact sheet: `race/preview_race.png` (3x on #141518). It shows two pit-stop mocks (car layers + crew, red tint), every crew sprite, the tyres, a world-scale pit lane mock, the start lights off / 3 lit / 5 lit, a podium scene and the HUD icons.

Import: Filter = Nearest, Mipmaps off, lossless, like the rest of `sprites/`. Strips need `texture_repeat = enabled`.
Light comes from the top-left everywhere. Coordinates are pixel corners, y down.

## Pit crew (10 px/m)

**Layers.** Each crew sprite is a `_paint` + `_detail` pair, the same contract as the car:
1. Draw `_paint` with `modulate` = the team colour. It holds the overalls (and the jack body) as the car's grey ramp, plus the baked drop shadow (black at 45 %, offset +2,+2, the same tone as `car/shadow.png`). Black survives the multiply, so the shadow stays correct for any team.
2. **Carriers only:** draw `tyre_held_<compound>.png` centred on `anchors.wheel_center`. If the carrier is flipped, flip the wheel too. Use the compound being fitted for the "tyre on" carriers and the old compound for the "tyre off" carriers.
3. Draw `_detail` untinted. It holds the white helmet, gloves, boots, wheel gun, jack fittings, lollipop pole and sign, the 1 px outline (#1d1f23) and the warm lift on the brightest paint pixels.

**Orientation.** The gunner and carrier face +y: they stand on the car's -y side and look down at it. For the +y side, `flip_v` the whole sprite and mirror anchor y as `y' = height - y`. The baked light then comes from the bottom-left; that is the same trade-off `car/` makes with `flip_v` on its lower front tyre. `crew_jack_front` and the lollipop face -x, and `crew_jack_rear` faces +x.

**Frames.** `_a` / `_b` for the gunner and carrier, and `stop` / `go` for the lollipop. Flip between a and b at 4-6 fps, and give neighbouring crew opposite frames so they don't move in lockstep.

**Pit-stop layout.** `crew.json` → `pit_stop_layout` gives 15 placements relative to the car centre (pixel corner (40, 16) of the car canvas): four gunners on the wheel nuts, four tyre-on carriers outboard of the wheels, four tyre-off carriers between the wheels, two jacks and the lollipop. Each placement puts the named anchor on the point.
- Points used: gunners at (±18, ∓10), the outer edge of each tyre; jacks at (30, 0) (nose) and (-29, 0) (rear crash structure).
- Layer `under_car` (the jacks) means draw after `car/shadow.png` and before the car floor, so the jack heads disappear under the nose and the gearbox.
- Everything else is `over_car`.
- To jack the car up, you can scale the car (not the crew) by about 1.03 or nudge its shadow 1 px further out.

## Generator

`tools/art/race.py` imports `pixel.py` (canvas, outline, noise, the 5 px font, kerb/steel/concrete ramps) and `pxl.py` (the car's paint, carbon, tyre, metal and visor ramps, multiply). Both helper files are unchanged.
- Crew are ASCII part grids drawn facing +y and rotated before shading.
- A part's edges facing the top-left light go up one ramp step, and edges facing away go down one.
- A part lying under a higher part (an arm over the back) gets a one-step contact shadow on its lit side.
- To change a pose, edit the grid. To change a material, edit `MATERIALS`.
